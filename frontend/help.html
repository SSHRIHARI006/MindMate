FAQs

Q: Is it okay to seek help?
A: Absolutely. Mental health is just as important as physical health. Seeking help is a brave and important step towards recovery.

Q: Will my data be safe?
A: Yes. We do not store or share any personal information you provide. Your privacy is our priority.

Q: What if I don't feel ready to talk to someone?
A: It's okay to feel unsure. You can start by journaling, practicing self-care, or reaching out to online resources to help you feel more comfortable.

Q: How can I manage stress during exams?
A: Try techniques like deep breathing, meditation, and taking regular breaks. Prioritize sleep and healthy meals to keep your energy levels up.

Q: What are the first signs of depression?
A: Common signs include persistent sadness, loss of interest in daily activities, changes in sleep or appetite, fatigue, and thoughts of hopelessness.

Q: How can meditation help with mental health?
A: Meditation helps to calm your mind, reduce stress, and improve emotional well-being. It promotes mindfulness and self-awareness, which can help manage anxiety and depression.

Q: How can I help a friend who seems depressed?
A: Be a good listener, offer emotional support, and encourage them to seek professional help. Let them know they are not alone and that it's okay to seek assistance.

Q: How do I find a therapist near me?
A: You can use our location service to find mental health professionals nearby or visit websites like Practo or TherapyRoute to explore options in your area.
